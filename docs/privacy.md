# Virtus privacy policy

Virtus does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Your name, the quotes you pin and your settings are stored only on your device, in a container shared
  with the app's widgets. They are never sent anywhere.
- The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.
- The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

If you delete the app, everything it stored is deleted with it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/jxmils/virtus.

Last updated 15 October 2026.
