## User reference captions

- The existing “I will either find a way or make one” keeps its text and ID, but its
  source now says “Traditionally attributed; not verified in an ancient source.”
  The old “Crossing the Alps” label asserted a setting without evidence. See the
  [attribution discussion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inveniam_viam).
- “My enemies are many, my equals are none” was not added as a historical Napoleon
  quotation. The wording appears as dialogue in Napoleon: Total War; see the
  [game attribution](https://www.quotes.net/quote/72580).
- “Until breath leaves my body, defeat is just a rumour” was not added under
  Alexander's name: no ancient source was established in this review.

New authors were not introduced, so all additions use the existing artwork mapping.
